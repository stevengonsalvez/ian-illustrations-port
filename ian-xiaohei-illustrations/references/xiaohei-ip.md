# Blot IP

## Character definition

Blot is the fixed visual IP for Ian-style article body illustrations.

By default, Blot appears in every image. Blot is not a mascot, not a sticker, not cute decoration — Blot is an absurd worker who is earnestly taking part in keeping the system running.

## Appearance

- A small solid-black creature.
- White round dot eyes.
- Thin legs, occasionally thin arms.
- The body can be a cylinder, a black bean, a black box, a funnel, a shadow, a hole mouth, or a black block inside a machine.
- The outline is slightly irregular, with a hand-drawn feel.
- The expression is blank, deadpan, calm, earnest.

## Personality

- Very earnest, but doing something slightly absurd.
- Like a low-key system operator.
- Dry humor, never plays cute.
- A bit clumsy, but not dumb.
- Like genuinely being in charge of some job inside a whiteboard sketch.

## Common duties

Have Blot carry the core action:

- Hauling materials.
- Pulling lines to converge information sources.
- Stuck inside a breakpoint.
- Operating a "judgment" lever inside a machine.
- Becoming a filtering funnel.
- Slicing the "material fish."
- Stamping a handoff line of copy.
- Leading a handoff path by hand.
- Holding a warning sign while looking at a pit.
- Reaching out of a hole but failing to catch the content.
- Off to the side hauling bricks, building bridges, opening doors, sorting, taking notes.

## Forbidden

- Do not draw Blot as an over-cute mascot.
- Do not draw it as a children's cartoon character.
- Do not give Blot complex clothing, emoji faces, or shiny eyes.
- Do not let Blot just stand in a corner watching.
- Do not let Blot steal the structural expression.
- Do not draw Blot too commercial, too round, too polished.

## Litmus test

If you remove Blot and the image's core metaphor still fully holds, that means Blot was too decorative; rewrite the prompt so Blot becomes the acting subject.
